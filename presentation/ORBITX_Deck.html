The Problem & the Data Stack

Earth is 15–22 light-minutes away. No GPS. Dust, radiation, steep slopes.
  • MRO HiRISE DTM (1,132,096 vertices, SHA-256 verified) + MOLA
  • USGS SIM 3292: 44 geologic units with trafficability
  • Perseverance (559 legs, 22 tubes), Ingenuity (72 flights), Curiosity, MEDA, RAD, DONKI
Honest limit: terrain resolution cannot resolve individual rocks. Panels tag measured data vs. simulation.

Space Weather & EVA Survival Radius

Beer-Lambert: I = 586.2 · exp(−τ / sin(elevation))
  • Dust optical depth τ from 0.45 (nominal) to 2.80 (global storm)
  • Point-of-No-Return EVA radius contracts with visibility and suit O2
  • Storm triage: GCR 0.67 mSv/day and DONKI SEP alerts
Atmospheric and solar values are a simulation parameterized by MEDA/RAD data.
